Request for Burial Space

Who May Avail: Mandaue City Residents | Classification: Simple (G2C)

Checklist of Requirements Where to Secure
1. Death Certificate (1 copy) City Health Office
2. Barangay Certification (1 copy) Barangay concerned
3. Exhumation and Reburial Permit (1 copy) City Health Office

Client Steps & Process:

  1. Submit Request: Submit letter request to the receiving clerk. (Fee: None | Processing Time: 5 mins | Person Responsible: Section Head)
  2. Verification: Office verifies logbook for burial space availability and verifies with cemetery in-charge. (Fee: None | Processing Time: 20 mins)
  3. Certification Issuance: Office issues Certification and forwards to Section Head. (Fee: None | Processing Time: 8 hours)
  4. Receive Certification: Receive (1) original copy of the Certification; office retains file copy. (Fee: None | Processing Time: 5 mins)
Total Fees: NONE | Total Processing Time: 1 Day, 30 Minutes

Plot Lease Renewal Requirements:

  • Original Certificate of Plot Allocation / Deed
  • Government-issued ID of Deed Holder
  • Latest Official Receipt of Annual Lease/Maintenance Fee

Visitor & Cleaning Guidelines:

  • Grounds open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Clean as you go (CLAYGO) policy strictly enforced
  • No loud sound systems or alcoholic beverages allowed
